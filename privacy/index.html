Prism Drop

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Prism Drop is made by Sandybyte. For privacy questions or requests, email support@sandybyte.com.

Your game data

Your scores, daily challenge results, shards, themes, goals, discovered prisms and settings are saved on your device. There is no Prism Drop account, cloud sync or third-party gameplay analytics in this version. Removing the app removes its local data from your device, subject to copies in your device backups.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your best scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; you can manage it in iOS Settings.

Advertising and privacy choices

Prism Drop is free to play with ads. The app uses Google AdMob to show rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example to revive a run or earn bonus shards) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. Before ads are requested, Google's consent tool shows privacy choices where the law requires them, including in the UK and EEA, and you can accept, decline or manage your options.

After you have played, the app may ask for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalised. Allowing tracking never increases the number of ads you see.

When ads are shown, Google may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address and approximate location, crash and performance data, ads shown, and interactions such as video views. Google may use information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and improving its services, and may work with advertising partners.

You can change your choices at any time from Ad Privacy Choices in Settings when that option applies, and from iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps using its services and Google's advertising information for details and controls.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily challenge reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to third parties. You can turn it off in the app's Settings or in your device settings.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases of Remove Forced Ads, the Prism Supporter Pack and shard packs. The app checks verified StoreKit transactions to unlock purchases and add shards; it does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase records under Apple's privacy policy.

Contacting us and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include so we can respond. Please do not send sensitive information. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses to operate and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Prism Drop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can review or change advertising and tracking choices, turn reminders on or off, or delete local app data as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct or delete personal information held about you. Contact us at the address above; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date at the top tells you when it was last updated.
