Prism Drop

How can we help?

Questions, feedback or a prism that isn't behaving? We're here to help.

Contact Sandybyte

Email support@sandybyte.com. Please include your device, iOS version and a description of the problem. A screenshot can help; please leave out anything private.

How do I play?

Drag to aim and release to drop a prism. When two identical prisms touch they merge into the next tier. Keep the stack below the line: if it stays above the line for too long, the run ends.

What is the Daily Challenge?

Everyone gets the same sequence of prisms each day (reset at midnight UTC). Play it once a day to build your streak and compare scores on the Game Center leaderboard.

Where is my progress saved?

Your scores, shards, themes and goals are stored on your device. Deleting the app can remove that data. The game does not have its own account or cloud sync.

How do optional videos work?

Some buttons show a play badge. If you choose to watch a short video you can revive a run, shake the container, swap the next prism, double the shards from a run or earn bonus shards. If no video is available right now, try again a little later. Occasional full-screen ads may appear between runs; purchasing Remove Forced Ads or the Supporter Pack turns those off.

A purchase isn't showing

Check that you are signed in with the Apple Account used to buy, then open Settings in Prism Drop and choose Restore Purchases. An internet connection may be needed. If it still isn't showing, contact us with the details above.

How do I change my advertising privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and EEA), open Settings in Prism Drop and choose Ad Privacy Choices. You can also turn off tracking for the app in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
